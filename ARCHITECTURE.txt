  ├─ Max value? 99,999,999.99
  └─ Format? Decimal (up to 2 places)

      'price' => 'nullable|numeric|min:0',
      // ... other rules
  ]);

  DECIMAL(10, 2)
  - Total digits: 10
  - Decimal places: 2
  - Range: 0.00 to 99,999,999.99

───────────────

Input Validation:
  5000      ✅ Valid
